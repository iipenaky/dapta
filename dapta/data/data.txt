The AphasiaBank corpus used in this research cannot be redistributed as it is subject to a restricted research-use agreement. Access must be obtained directly from the AphasiaBank project.
To request access, visit: https://aphasia.talkbank.org
Once access is granted, place the English aphasia transcripts under data/aphasiabank/ as described in documentation/readme.txt.
Citation: MacWhinney, B., Fromm, D., Forbes, M., & Holland, A. (2011). AphasiaBank: Methods for studying discourse. Aphasiology, 25(11), 1286–1307.
